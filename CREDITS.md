## When you publish the mod

Players get the mod from the Workshop, not from this repository. So the
credits have to be on the Workshop page too. Put this in the mod description:

```text
Third-party assets (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
- Apple & Plum: "Lowpoly Fruits & Vegetables" by Loïc Norgeot (https://skfb.ly/6z6Ls), converted for Arma Reforger
- Car Key: "Electronic Car Key" by neeb17 (https://skfb.ly/6UYyH), converted for Arma Reforger
These assets are not covered by the mod's APL-SA license.
```
